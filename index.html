How a CRRT circuit clears things

tap to open · 90 seconds
blooddialysate · diffusionreplacement · convection

Two ways to clear: diffusion runs dialysate past the membrane so small solutes (K+, Na+, urea, H+) follow their gradient across. Convection pushes replacement fluid through the blood so water drags solutes (including middle molecules) with it. The modality name just says which fluid lines are running.

  • CVVHDdialysate only · pure diffusion · best for a single small-solute problem (K+, Na+)
  • CVVHreplacement only · pure convection · volume and middle molecules, no dialysate for K+
  • CVVHDFboth lines · the default when you want everything

Evidence: when dose is matched, none of the three changes mortality or renal recovery (network meta-analyses 2021; Barbar 2025). Modality is chosen for the problem, not for superiority.
